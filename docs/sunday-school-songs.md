# Sunday School songs — 17 October 2026

Added `/collections/sunday-school/` using the existing collection cards, song reader, printing, search, and personal setlists. It appears first in the collection directory. The six existing homepage collection cards remain in place.

## Selection

Eight existing Telugu entries: Chitti Potti Paapanu; Chinna Gorrepillanu Nenu; Chinna Chinna Gorrepillanu; Chinni Chinni Chethulatho; Chinni Manasutho Ninnu; Naa Chinni Hrudayamandu; Naalaanti Chinnalante; Pillalaaraa. Membership is explicitly selected, rather than inferred from every occurrence of “little” or “child.” No Telugu source lyrics were rewritten or copied from a competitor.

Three new English entries, each generating its own `/song/{slug}/` page:

- **Jesus Loves Me (Children’s Chorus)** — Anna Bartlett Warner; refrain by William B. Bradbury. First verse and refrain only, labelled as a children’s chorus rather than a complete multi-verse edition. [1910 public-domain text and tune record](https://hymnary.org/hymn/PH31910/230).
- **Jesus Loves the Little Children (Chorus)** — C. H. Woolston. The traditional standalone refrain, preserving the historical wording. [Public-domain text and version](https://hymnary.org/text/jesus_loves_the_little_children_all_the).
- **Jesus Bids Us Shine** — Susan Warner. Three-verse text. [Public-domain text](https://hymnary.org/text/jesus_bids_us_shine_with_a_pure_clear_li).

Only the public-domain words were added, not modern arrangements, recordings, translations, scores, or site editorial material. Keertana does not claim authorship. The competitor’s [kids directory](https://christiantelugulyrics.com/kids-songs/) was used to understand the category; Naalaanti Chinnalante is already present in Keertana.
